Demo · ProjecTTrone · 2D mini-game

ProjecTTrone Lite

The mechanics I coded in the 3D game, rebuilt in 2D for the browser: explore the medieval town, pick up items spotted by a ray (“Press E”), manage the inventory (I key) and open the throne room.

Graphics drawn in code for the demo: the original project’s licensed assets are not published.

This demo needs JavaScript to run. The rest of the portfolio stays readable without it.

Objective

Inventory 0/5

    Picked-up items show here. Data (ItemData) and world items (Item) are kept apart, as in the Unity project.

    Controls

    • Arrows or WASD: move.
    • E: pick up the targeted item, open the gate, sit on the throne.
    • I: show or hide the inventory.
    • On mobile: the pad and buttons under the game.
